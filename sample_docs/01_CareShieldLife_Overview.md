# CareShield Life overview
Source agency: Ministry of Health (MOH)
Source URL: https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/careshield-life/careshield-life/
Checked: 13 November 2026

CareShield Life is Singapore's long-term care insurance scheme. For Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents born in 1980 or later, coverage is automatic when they turn 30, or from 1 October 2020, whichever is later. For people born in 1979 or earlier, participation is generally optional, subject to specific rules for some groups and residency circumstances.

MOH states that Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents born from 1970 to 1979 who were insured under ElderShield 400 and did not have severe disability were automatically enrolled from 1 December 2021. Those not automatically enrolled may apply if they meet the applicable conditions. The rules changed from 1 January 2026: people born in 1979 or earlier applying to join must not have pre-existing disabilities. Users must confirm their own status through CPF Board and the official application service.

CareShield Life provides monthly cash payouts for as long as the insured remains severely disabled under the scheme's assessment criteria. Payout amounts change over time; do not assume a historic launch amount is the current amount. CPF Board states the 2026 monthly payout for existing eligible policyholders is $689 and that payouts increase annually until age 67 or a successful claim, whichever is earlier. Always verify the applicable cohort and policy-year details on CPF Board's current pages.

CareShield Life premiums can be paid using MediSave. Personalised premiums and support depend on individual circumstances and should be checked through CPF Board's CareShield Life Premium Checker. NaviCare must not estimate an individual's premium from birth year alone.
