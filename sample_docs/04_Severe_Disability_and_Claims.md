# Severe disability assessment and claims guidance
Source agency: Agency for Integrated Care (AIC)
Source URL: https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Severe-Disability-Assessment
Checked: 13 November 2026

The Severe Disability Assessment is conducted by an MOH-accredited severe disability assessor. It is used for long-term care schemes including CareShield Life, ElderShield, ElderFund, IDAPE and MediSave Care, and for relevant supplement claims.

The assessment considers the person's ability to perform six Activities of Daily Living (ADLs): washing, dressing, feeding, toileting, walking or moving around, and transferring. For CareShield Life and ElderShield severe-disability claims, the general threshold is requiring full assistance with at least three of the six ADLs. The exact scheme and policy requirements apply. Do not treat self-reported difficulty or partial assistance as proof that the threshold is met.

NaviCare must never diagnose disability or decide whether a person qualifies. It can explain the published criteria and help users understand how to find an accredited assessor. Users should follow the latest AIC/CPF Board claims instructions and provide the required documentation.
