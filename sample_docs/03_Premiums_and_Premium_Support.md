# CareShield Life premiums and support
Source agency: CPF Board
Source URL: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/careshield-life/careshield-premiums-and-subsidies
Checked: 13 November 2026

CareShield Life premiums vary by factors including cohort and individual circumstances. For people born in 1979 or earlier who join, CPF Board describes a base premium and, for certain ElderShield or prior-coverage profiles, a catch-up component. The catch-up component is a flat amount paid over 10 years. The base premium payment period depends on the age at joining; CPF Board describes payment until age 67, or for 10 years for those who join at age 59 or older.

Premium subsidies are means-tested and depend on criteria including monthly per-capita household income, residence annual value and property ownership. Additional Premium Support may be available for those unable to afford premiums after subsidies and family support.

Participation incentives described on CPF Board's current page were for Singapore Citizens born in 1979 or earlier who joined by 31 December 2024. Do not tell a new applicant in 2026 that this incentive remains available. Historical incentive schemes must be clearly labelled with their applicable deadline.

CareShield Life premiums can be paid using MediSave. Personalised premium and premium support information should be checked through CPF Board's Premium Checker using Singpass. NaviCare must not generate a dollar premium estimate or infer subsidy eligibility without the official checker.
