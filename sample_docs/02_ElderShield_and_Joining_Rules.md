# ElderShield 300/400 and joining CareShield Life
Source agency: CPF Board
Source URL: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/how-do-i-join-eldershield
Checked: 13 November 2026

ElderShield is no longer open for new applications. Existing ElderShield 300 coverage provides a monthly cash payout of $300 for up to 60 months, while ElderShield 400 provides $400 per month for up to 72 months, subject to policy terms and claim eligibility.

People born in 1979 or earlier who do not have disability may be able to apply to join CareShield Life, subject to the rules effective from 1 January 2026. People born in 1980 or later are automatically covered when they turn 30, subject to the scheme rules.

For some Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents born from 1970 to 1979 who were insured under ElderShield 400 and did not have severe disability, automatic enrolment to CareShield Life took effect on 1 December 2021. Historical opt-out deadlines and free-look periods are cohort- and circumstance-specific. Do not generalise an old deadline to every person; check the person's official CPF coverage record and current CPF Board guidance.

A person who has developed disability may not be eligible to newly join CareShield Life. If already insured under ElderShield and has not claimed, ElderShield claim eligibility may still need to be considered. An accredited assessment and the exact policy terms apply.
